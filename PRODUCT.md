# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

SHSID international high-school students who are comparing academic competitions, contests, and selective summer programs while balancing school deadlines, subject interests, and college-application planning.

## Product Purpose

Project Pursuit turns school-provided lists and reproducible web snapshots into a China-relevant factual catalog. Success means a student can filter opportunities by published facts, distinguish missing information from verified information, and trace every populated field to retained evidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, credible, and calm. The product should feel like a trusted research console rather than a promotional directory.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic competition-list websites, oversized marketing heroes, decorative dashboards, opaque AI scoring, hidden uncertainty, and admissions-value claims that look deterministic.

## Design Principles

- Show provenance before persuasion.
- Make uncertainty visible instead of guessing.
- Optimize for comparison and decision-making.
- Keep recommendation logic explainable.
- Prefer dense, readable product UI over decorative layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, reduced motion, responsive mobile layouts, and bilingual Chinese/English competition names where source data provides both.
